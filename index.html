Because caring about someone means wanting them to be happy, even when life doesn't turn out exactly the way we imagined.

May this new year of your life bring you closer to your dreams. May Allah open doors of opportunities for you, protect you from what is harmful, surround you with genuine people, and bless you with a life full of love, peace, success, and barakah.
